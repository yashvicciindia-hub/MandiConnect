What is Mandi Connect?

A simple, no nonsense way to connect farmers and buyers directly

Mandi Connect is a free directory of India's vegetable mandis. Farmers can check today's rates before they sell, buyers can find a mandi and trader near them, and both sides can post what they're looking to buy or sell, all in one place, in your language.

1. Find Your Mandi

Search by city or produce to see active mandis near you, along with trader counts and what's traded there.

2. Check Today's Rates

Browse sample daily min to max prices for 50+ vegetables so you know a fair price before you buy or sell.

3. Connect Directly

Register as a farmer or buyer and get matched with people looking to trade, with no middleman guesswork.
